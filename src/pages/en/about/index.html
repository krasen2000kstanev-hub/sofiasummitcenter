About

A calm, professional venue for your next meeting

Sofia Summit Center is a modern conference center in Studentski Grad, Sofia, built for conferences, training, meetings and specialized formats.

We create a calm and professional setting for meetings, ideas and productive work.

  • Location

    13 "8-mi Dekemvri" Str., Studentski Grad, Sofia, in a quiet spot next to the large park. View on Google Maps.

  • Amenities

    Parking, a reception and a waiting lobby.

  • Ideal for

    Conferences, training, meetings, podcast recordings and presentations.
