FAQ

Before you book

The most common questions about the halls, services and bookings.

How do I book a date?

Send an inquiry through the form or call +359 894 202 086. We will get back to you with availability and an offer suited to your event.

How much does it cost?

The event hall starts at €350, the meeting room at €25 per hour and a podcast studio recording at €120. Prices exclude VAT. See all prices and calculate yours on the Prices page.

What is included in the price of the halls?

Wi-Fi, microphones, multimedia, a lectern, catering tables and lounge furniture. Time to set up and clear the hall is also included.

What kind of events do you host?

Conferences, training, seminars, product launches, cocktail receptions, award ceremonies, company parties, video calls and podcast recordings.

What is the difference between the meeting room and the podcast studio?

The meeting room has 15 seats and suits negotiations, training and video calls. The podcast studio is fully equipped for recordings, interviews and panels. See Spaces.

Do you have recording equipment?

Yes. The event hall and the podcast studio each have their own camera and sound systems, suited to the format. See Equipment.

What services do you offer?

Video and photo, editing, landing pages, event organization and a 360° photo booth. For the event hall we can also arrange catering and a DJ, by agreement. See Services.

Do you organize events from start to finish?

Yes. We help with planning, coordination, technical preparation and delivery. If needed, we can also connect you with our partners.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is available, along with a reception and a waiting lobby.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes, for many recordings in one day, larger quantities, a long-term partnership or using more than one service.

Can't find an answer?

Ask us directly and we will reply within the working day.
