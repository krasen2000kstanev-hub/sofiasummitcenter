What you get

The equipment in the hall covers the whole event, from the presentation to the recording.

  • Multimedia and sound

    A conference lectern with four fixed microphones, two lapel microphones, a stage and an 85-inch TV, a podium or stand microphones.

  • Stage and layout

    The stage and the hall are rearranged for your format: theatre, classroom, cocktail or banquet.

  • Cameras and sound

    Three remote-controlled ceiling cameras and a sound system covering the whole event.

  • Breakout rooms

    Three rooms for group work, plus parking, a reception and a waiting lobby for your guests.

  • Catering and DJ

    We can arrange catering and a DJ for your event, by agreement.
