    Good to know

    • Hall overstay: if the hall is not vacated and returned in the agreed condition by the end of the rental, the listed price is due for each started hour.
    • Service overtime: if a service continues after the agreed time, the listed price is due for each started hour.
    • After 9 pm: using an extra service after 21:00 costs another €20 for each started hour.
    • Damage: damage to a hall or equipment is charged at three times the cost of repair.
    • This price list is for information only and is not a binding offer.
